## Scope

The MixMusic landing page on GitHub Pages (`site/`), published at fzn0x.github.io/mixmusic. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: listeners who use Spotify and YouTube Music, and theme and plugin authors, arriving from GitHub, search and links. Action: download the app for their system from the latest release; starring the repository is the second action. Proof: real screenshots captured from the built app, the real registry (ten built-in themes, the community themes Neon Nights and Paper Mint, the plugins Sleep Timer and Spectrum), the MIT license, the release's files. Constraints: no testimonials, user counts or benchmarks; no other project named as a reference; Spotify and YouTube Music named, never imitated; a static site deployed by GitHub Actions; the app's own Selawik font.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page plays MixMusic like its own queue: the hero is now playing (the real app at work under a giant cropped "mixmusic", with the download), and every section waits as an up next row the visitor plays down the page. It refuses the centred headline over a row of feature cards and the split hero beside a device mockup.

OWN-WORLD: MixMusic's tile wall, inherited: true black ground, white Selawik at weight 300 for every large word, lowercase page words cropped by the edge, flat cyan, magenta, lime and amber tiles in black ink with dot-matrix print inside them only, one live cyan accent flooding what is current (the playing row, the download), 2px white outlines for secondary buttons, square corners, source marks (disc for Spotify, square for YouTube Music), panel black for the queue column and the player bar.

STORY: The visitor sees the player itself at work and reads in one line what it is: Spotify and YouTube Music in one free, open-source desktop player. They download from the player bar, or play down the queue: one search, one queue, one library, explore (new, charts, moods and genres, related), your accounts (YouTube Music, Spotify, Last.fm, Discord), lyrics and sound, at the keyboard (palette, shortcuts, tray, mini player), themes, plugins and the registry, built in the open, each opening onto real screenshots. They leave with the installer, and knowing MIT, GitHub, and that a theme or plugin is one pull request away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: at 1440 by 900, a 48px top bar (rings mark and "mixmusic", links to features, themes, plugins and docs, a GitHub outline button). The main column (about 70%) opens on a giant cropped lowercase "mixmusic" running past its edge, pivots peeking under it (one search, one queue, one library), and the real app screenshot large beneath as the now playing artwork. The right column, in panel black, is "up next": the first row floods cyan as now playing, then one queue row per section. A sticky 92px player bar runs along the bottom: the section in view as the track, a 2px accent progress line, and the primary action, a cyan "download for <your system>" block with an "other systems" outline beside it.

FORM: "Now playing, up next", position 4 of my seven grounded structures, dealt as the lead; seed key 86795213. Signature interaction: the player bar plays the page. Its progress line is the scroll position, its title turns to the section in view, the up next column advances with the playing row flooded in the accent, and the primary action stays Download throughout.
