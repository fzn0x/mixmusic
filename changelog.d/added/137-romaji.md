- Japanese lyrics can show in romaji: turn on "romaji" beside the lyrics,
  then choose "romaji under", each line with its romaji under it, or
  "romaji only", the romaji in place of the Japanese. Korean, Chinese and
  lyrics in other scripts than Latin letters get the same switch, named
  "romanize". With translate on too, a line shows the Japanese, its
  romaji, then its translation. The choice is remembered for the next
  song in the same language. The romaji comes from Unison, which has
  Google romanize the lyrics: while it's on, they go to Unison with the
  track's video ID (#137).
