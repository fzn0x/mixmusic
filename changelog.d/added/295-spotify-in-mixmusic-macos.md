- On macOS, MixMusic plays Spotify tracks itself, with no Spotify app
  open: choose In MixMusic, from Spotify in Settings › Playback. Spotify's
  own player plays them, at Spotify's quality, with FairPlay, macOS's own
  protection for Spotify's audio, and MixMusic shows among your Spotify
  devices. It needs Spotify Premium. On Spotify Connect stays the default
  there, and the visualizer can't hear it yet (#295).
