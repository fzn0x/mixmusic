- Two new design languages beside MixMusic's own, each light and dark.
  Material and Material Dark follow Material 3 Expressive: colors made of
  the cover that plays (with Follow the artwork, on by default), pill
  buttons and chips, large rounded cards, a floating player bar, Roboto
  Flex, and controls that move on springs. Crate and Crate Light are
  dense, with big covers in card grids, a compact sidebar and heavy
  headings. Pick them in Settings › Appearance. Themes can now set layout
  tokens too: their shapes, density, cover size, headings, player bar and
  motion, which docs/themes.md lists. Themes that set none, as every
  theme so far, look as before. MixMusic grows by about 0.5 MB with the
  two new typefaces (#68).
