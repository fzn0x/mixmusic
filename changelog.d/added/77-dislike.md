- Dislike songs you never want to hear again: with the thumbs-down beside
  the heart in the player bar, on the now playing page and in the mini
  player, with Dislike in every track's menu, or with Ctrl+D (⌘D on
  macOS). MixMusic then passes over them as the queue moves on, forward
  and back, moves on at once from the song you disliked as it plays, and
  leaves them out of radios; a toast's undo takes a dislike back and goes
  back to the song where it was. A song you pick yourself still plays,
  and the play buttons of albums, playlists, mixes and artists start at
  their first song you don't dislike. A song is liked or disliked, not
  both: disliking a liked song unlikes it, on Spotify too. Signed in to
  YouTube Music, dislikes of its songs go there too; Spotify takes none,
  so those stay in MixMusic. The library lists them in a new disliked
  pivot, the queue shows the songs it skips, and Settings › Playback turns
  the skipping off. The mini player's artwork is narrower, to make room
  for the button. Plugins get `api.library.isDisliked` and `setDisliked`
  (#77).
