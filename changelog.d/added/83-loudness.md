- Even loudness, in Settings › Playback and on by default: a YouTube Music
  track louder than the rest is turned down to one level from its first
  note, from YouTube's own measure of it, so none jumps out. Quiet
  recordings keep their level. Loud masters are common, and play 4 to
  7 dB quieter than before: turn up your system's volume, or turn Even
  loudness off, if they sound too quiet now. Once measured, tracks keep
  their level offline too, from the cache. Spotify tracks that Spotify
  plays keep Spotify's own loudness (#83).
