# Features
<!-- docs: group=start order=15 -->

Every feature of MixMusic, from 0.1.0 to 0.5.12, one line each: what it does and where to find it. "Built in" means it works by itself, with nothing to open or turn on. Each name links to the page that tells more. A feature of only one or two systems says which.

## Search and play

Find music, queue it and play it, with the controls that go with playing.

- **[Search both services](playing-music.md#search).** Searches Spotify and YouTube Music together, with suggested searches as you type and a list of your recent searches. *Top bar, Search* · since 0.1.0
- **[Choose where to search](playing-music.md#search).** Searches all music, Spotify's and YouTube Music's and your own files, or only Spotify or only YouTube Music. *Search* · since 0.1.0
- **[Song and album suggestions](playing-music.md#search).** Suggests songs, albums, artists and playlists from YouTube Music under the search box. A song plays at once. *Top bar* · since 0.5.10
- **[Search pivots](playing-music.md#search).** Narrows the results to songs, videos, albums, artists, playlists or podcasts, each with how many it found. *Search* · since 0.1.0
- **[Videos in search](playing-music.md#search).** Finds YouTube videos that are not songs of the music catalog, such as DJ sets, live recordings and fans' uploads. *Search* · since 0.5.10
- **[Search YouTube itself](playing-music.md#search).** Adds YouTube as a place to search, for tutorials, vlogs and live streams the music catalog lacks. All music is the default. *Search* · since 0.5.12
- **[Sort search results](playing-music.md#search).** Orders results by best match or by most played, and albums by best match or newest, from Sort by beside the services. *Search* · since 0.5.12
- **[Play counts in results](playing-music.md#search).** Shows how many times each YouTube Music song played, or how many views a video has, so the original stands out from covers. *Built in* · since 0.5.0
- **[Open a link](playing-music.md#open-a-link).** Paste a YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify link in the search box and Enter plays or opens what it names. *Top bar, Command palette* · since 0.5.12
- **[One queue for both services](playing-music.md#the-queue-and-up-next).** Plays tracks of Spotify and YouTube Music one after another from a single queue called Up next. *Queue* · since 0.1.0
- **[Add to queue in one step](playing-music.md#playing-from-anywhere).** A queue button on a row, and Shift+Enter or Alt+Enter on a focused one, adds a track to the queue or plays it next. *Library, Search, Home* · since 0.5.12
- **[Play next from Up next](playing-music.md#the-queue-and-up-next).** Moves a track already in Up next to right after the one playing. *Queue* · since 0.5.12
- **[Queue comes back after a restart](playing-music.md#the-queue-and-up-next).** Restores the queue on the track that played, where it was, with the tracks around it, and keeps changes made while paused. *Built in* · since 0.1.0
- **[Shuffle and repeat](playing-music.md#shuffle-and-repeat).** Shuffle mixes the tracks still to play, and Repeat plays the queue or the track again. *Player bar* · since 0.1.0
- **[Autoplay](playing-music.md#autoplay-and-radios).** Carries on with a radio of what you played when the queue runs out, starting its first song as the last one ends. *Settings › Playback › Sound* · since 0.1.0
- **[Start radio](playing-music.md#autoplay-and-radios).** Plays a track and then a radio of songs like it, kept apart from the songs you add. *A track's menu, An album's page* · since 0.1.0
- **[Album, artist and playlist pages](playing-music.md#playing-from-anywhere).** Open the page of an album, artist or playlist, with Play and Shuffle and a More menu of what to do with it. *A track's menu, Search, Home* · since 0.1.0
- **[Play all of an artist's songs](playing-music.md#all-of-an-artists-songs).** Play and Shuffle on an artist's page or tile play all their songs, not only the top ones, on Spotify those of all their albums and singles. *An artist's page, A tile's menu* · since 0.5.12
- **Related music.** Shows related songs, playlists and artists at the foot of the now playing page. *Now playing* · since 0.2.0
- **[Select several tracks](playing-music.md#selecting-tracks).** Ctrl-click or Shift-click rows to select tracks, then add them to the queue or a mix, or download them, in one go. *Library, Search* · since 0.5.11
- **[Track info](playing-music.md#track-info).** Tells where a track comes from, its format, bitrate and file, and how MixMusic found its stream. *A track's menu* · since 0.5.11
- **[Details of a failed track](troubleshooting.md#tracks-that-fail-to-play).** A track that couldn't play has Details in its toast, listing what MixMusic tried in turn and why each try failed, ready to copy for an issue. *A failed track's toast, Settings › About › Recent playback errors* · since 0.5.11
- **[Why a track could not play](troubleshooting.md#tracks-that-fail-to-play).** Says in plain words why a track can't play, and stops the queue only after three in a row fail. *Built in* · since 0.3.0
- **[Copy title, artist or link](playing-music.md#sharing).** Copies a song's link, title, artist or "Artist - Title" from its menu. *A track's menu* · since 0.5.11
- **[Copy link of albums and playlists](playing-music.md#sharing).** Copies the link of an album, playlist or artist on its service, from its page or tile. *An album's page, Library* · since 0.5.0
- **[Share as an image card](playing-music.md#sharing).** Saves a square or tall card of a song, album, playlist or mix with its cover, title and artist, in your theme's colours. *A track's menu, An album's page* · since 0.5.11
- **[One tile for an album's versions](playing-music.md#versions-of-an-album).** Shows one tile for an album however many explicit, clean or deluxe versions the service lists, with a switch on its page. *An artist's page, Search, Library* · since 0.5.11
- **[Prefer clean versions](playing-music.md#versions-of-an-album).** Shows and opens the clean version of an album that came out explicit and clean. *Settings › Playback › Explicit content* · since 0.5.11
- **[Hide explicit content](playing-music.md#hiding-explicit-content).** Keeps songs, albums and videos marked explicit out of lists and radios, and never plays or downloads them. *Settings › Playback › Explicit content* · since 0.5.11
- **[Dislike songs](playing-music.md#dislikes-and-skipping).** Marks songs you never want to hear again, so the queue and radios pass over them. Ctrl+D dislikes what plays. *Player bar, A track's menu* · since 0.5.9
- **[Skip songs made with AI](playing-music.md#skipping-songs-made-with-ai).** Passes over songs YouTube labels as made with AI when the queue moves on by itself. *Settings › Playback › Sound* · since 0.5.9
- **[Skip parts with SponsorBlock](playing-music.md#skipping-parts-of-videos).** Skips the intros, sponsor reads and non-music parts of videos that SponsorBlock's community marks, with a toast to undo. *Settings › Playback › Skip parts of videos* · since 0.5.12
- **[Jump to the highlight](playing-music.md#skipping-parts-of-videos).** Plays a video from the moment SponsorBlock calls its best, and marks skipped parts and the highlight on the seek bar. *Now playing, Command palette* · since 0.5.12
- **[Live streams](playing-music.md#live-streams).** Plays YouTube live streams, such as 24/7 radio channels, and shows live in place of a time. *Player bar* · since 0.5.9
- **[Video of music videos](playing-music.md#the-picture-of-music-videos).** Shows a music video's picture on the now playing page in place of the cover, muted and in time with the sound. *Settings › Playback › Music videos, Now playing* · since 0.5.9
- **[Moving covers](playing-music.md#moving-covers).** Moves a GIF cover in the now playing page's sleeve, pausing with the music, and in the head of a mix's page. *Settings › Playback › Moving covers* · since 0.5.12
- **[Sleep timer](playing-music.md#a-sleep-timer).** Pauses the music after a time, at the end of the track or the queue, or after a number of tracks, fading it out first. *Player bar, Mini player, Tray menu, Command palette* · since 0.5.12
- **[Start the sleep timer by itself](playing-music.md#a-sleep-timer).** Starts the sleep timer at its last length when music starts inside a night window, the same every day or set per weekday. *Settings › Playback › Sleep timer* · since 0.5.12
- **[Sleep timer fade](playing-music.md#a-sleep-timer).** Sets how long the music fades out before the sleep timer pauses it, from off to 120 seconds. *Settings › Playback › Sleep timer* · since 0.5.12
- **[Speed](playing-music.md#speed).** Plays songs from half speed to three times, at their own pitch, from a button, a slider or commands. *Player bar, Settings › Playback › Speed* · since 0.5.9
- **[Waits for the network](playing-music.md#without-the-network).** When the network drops, the song waits for it and plays on from where it stopped, and retries a stalled stream. *Built in* · since 0.5.6
- **[Pauses when the computer sleeps](playing-music.md#without-the-network).** Pauses as the computer goes to sleep and stays paused when it wakes, ready where it was. *Built in* · since 0.5.6
- **[Tracks start in a moment](streams.md#youtubes-player-api).** YouTube Music tracks start almost at once, and the next track is found ahead of time. *Built in* · since 0.5.6

## Home and Explore

The pages that suggest music before you search.

- **[Home](home.md#what-it-shows).** Opens on tiles and shelves of your own listening, of YouTube Music's home feed and of Spotify, with pivots for each. *Home* · since 0.1.0
- **[Jump back in](home.md#what-it-shows).** A tile at the top of Home that plays the track you played last again. *Home* · since 0.1.0
- **[New from artists you follow](home.md#what-it-shows).** Shows a shelf of the albums and singles of the last eight weeks from the artists you follow. *Home, Library › Artists* · since 0.5.10
- **[Play and Shuffle on shelves](home.md#what-it-shows).** A shelf of songs has Play and Shuffle buttons in its title row that queue the songs in order or shuffled. *Home, Explore, Now playing* · since 0.5.12
- **[Edit home](home.md#arrange-home).** Drags Home's shelves into your order, hides the ones you don't want and brings them back. *Home* · since 0.5.11
- **[Add a shelf to Home](home.md#arrange-home).** Adds a shelf of one of your mixes, a library playlist, an artist's new releases, Most played this month or your downloads. *Home* · since 0.5.11
- **[Reset home](home.md#arrange-home).** Puts Home back as it comes, with the shelves you hid shown and those you added taken off. *Home* · since 0.5.11
- **[Home says when shelves fail](home.md#what-it-shows).** Shows your own shelves at once, and says why a service's shelves couldn't load, with a try again button. *Home* · since 0.5.8
- **[Explore: New](home.md#explore).** Shows YouTube Music's new albums and singles and its trending songs, with no sign-in. *Explore* · since 0.2.0
- **[Explore: Charts](home.md#explore).** Shows YouTube Music's charts. *Explore* · since 0.2.0
- **[Explore: Moods and genres](home.md#explore).** Opens a grid of moods and genres, each with its own playlists. *Explore* · since 0.2.0

## Lyrics

Lyrics for the track that plays, from several sources, in the lyrics panel and on the now playing page.

- **[Synced lyrics](lyrics.md#synced-and-plain-lyrics).** Shows lyrics that follow the song, lighting the line sung now. A click on a line plays the song from there. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.1.0
- **[Word-by-word lyrics](lyrics.md#word-by-word).** Lights each word of the line sung now as it's sung, for lyrics timed word by word. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.9
- **[Plain text lyrics](lyrics.md#synced-and-plain-lyrics).** Shows synced lyrics as plain text instead, without following the song. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.2.0
- **[Full-screen lyrics](lyrics.md#full-screen-lyrics).** Covers the window with the lyrics over the blurred cover, with the player's buttons at the left and the next covers at the right. *Lyrics panel, Now playing, Command palette, Keyboard* · since 0.5.12
- **[Lyrics style and bouncing ball](lyrics.md#full-screen-lyrics).** Chooses the full-screen lyrics' style, text size and a ball that hops from word to word, with a preview. *Settings › Appearance › Lyrics style* · since 0.5.12
- **[Translate lyrics](lyrics.md#translate).** Shows a translation under each line of lyrics in another language, in 47 languages you can choose. *Lyrics panel, Settings › Playback › Lyrics* · since 0.5.9
- **[Romaji](lyrics.md#japanese-lyrics-in-romaji).** Shows Japanese lyrics in Latin letters under the line or in its place. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.9
- **[Romanize other scripts](lyrics.md#other-scripts-in-latin-letters).** Shows lyrics in Korean, Chinese, Russian, Arabic and many other scripts in Latin letters, made on your computer, but for Arabic's, which come from Unison once it has them. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.9
- **[Find in the lyrics](lyrics.md#find-in-the-lyrics).** Finds the lines that hold the words you type, steps through them and plays the song from the one found. *Lyrics panel, Now playing, Command palette* · since 0.5.12
- **[Share lines of lyrics](lyrics.md#share-lines).** Saves the lines you choose as a square or tall image card with the song's cover, title and artist. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.11
- **[Lyrics sources](lyrics.md#choose-and-order-the-sources).** Asks Better Lyrics, Unison, LRCLIB, YouTube Music and YouTube captions in turn. You switch each off and set their order. *Settings › Playback › Lyrics* · since 0.5.12
- **[Prefer word-by-word lyrics](lyrics.md#choose-and-order-the-sources).** Lets lyrics timed word by word from a lower source beat lyrics timed line by line from a higher one. *Settings › Playback › Lyrics* · since 0.5.12
- **[YouTube captions as lyrics](lyrics.md#where-they-come-from).** Uses the timed captions of the playing video as lyrics for live versions and uploads no lyrics service has. *Settings › Playback › Lyrics* · since 0.5.12
- **[Pick another source](lyrics.md#pick-another-source).** Chooses which source's lyrics show for a song, with what each source answered, and keeps the pick. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.12
- **[Search lyrics by title and artist](lyrics.md#pick-another-source).** Asks every source for a title and artist you can change, and lists each answer so you can show one. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.12
- **[Edit lyrics](lyrics.md#pick-another-source).** Opens the lyrics as text with their LRC times so you can correct a word or a line, and keeps your edit. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.12
- **[Lyrics timing for a song](lyrics.md#timing).** Earlier and Later arrows move one song's lyrics by 0.05 s, or 1 s with Shift, and the timing is kept for it. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.12
- **[Lyrics timing for every song](lyrics.md#timing).** Moves the lyrics of every song earlier or later, for an output that sounds late such as Bluetooth headphones. *Settings › Playback › Lyrics* · since 0.5.12
- **[Lyrics timed to music videos](lyrics.md#music-videos).** Finds where a song starts in its music video and moves its lyrics there, also when the video plays it a little slower. *Built in* · since 0.5.12
- **[Lyrics of songs on this computer](lyrics.md#songs-on-this-computer).** Shows the lyrics a song file holds, or a .lrc file beside it, at once and offline. *Built in* · since 0.5.12
- **[Lyrics credits](lyrics.md#where-they-come-from).** Ends the lyrics with where they came from and, for Unison's, who submitted them, linked where the source has a page. *Built in* · since 0.5.9

## Podcasts

Shows and episodes of YouTube Music and Spotify, kept apart from songs.

- **[Find podcasts](podcasts.md#find-shows-and-episodes).** Finds shows and episodes of YouTube Music and Spotify in the search, under its Podcasts pivot. *Search* · since 0.5.9
- **[A show's page](podcasts.md#a-shows-page).** Lists a show's episodes newest first with their days, lengths and how much is left, and a Play or Resume button. *A show's page* · since 0.5.9
- **[Start where you left off](podcasts.md#where-you-left-off).** Starts an episode from where you left it, in MixMusic or on another device, whatever played before. *Built in* · since 0.5.9
- **[Follow shows](podcasts.md#following-shows).** Follows a show in MixMusic, and on YouTube Music or Spotify too when you're signed in to it. *A show's page* · since 0.5.9
- **[Keep listening](podcasts.md#following-shows).** Lists the episodes you left partway and the shows you follow or saved on a service. *Library › Podcasts* · since 0.5.9
- **[Mark as played](podcasts.md#an-episodes-menu).** Marks an episode as played or unplayed from its menu. *An episode's menu* · since 0.5.9
- **[Continue on your phone](podcasts.md#with-youtube-music).** Signed in to YouTube Music, episodes start where your other devices left them and tell YouTube Music how far you got. *Built in* · since 0.5.9
- **[Spotify's episodes](podcasts.md#spotifys-episodes).** Plays Spotify's podcast episodes from Spotify itself, in MixMusic on Windows and macOS, or on Spotify Connect. It needs Premium. *A show's page* · since 0.5.9
- **[Episode details and chapters](podcasts.md#episode-details-and-chapters).** Shows an episode's show, views, date and description where a song shows lyrics. Times play from there and the chapter lights up. *Lyrics panel, Now playing* · since 0.5.9
- **[Episode transcript](podcasts.md#episode-details-and-chapters).** Shows the captions of a YouTube Music episode under its details as a timed transcript, where a click plays from there. *Now playing* · since 0.5.12
- **[Podcast speed](podcasts.md#speed).** Plays episodes at a speed of their own, apart from songs. *Settings › Playback › Speed, Player bar* · since 0.5.9
- **[Episodes play without crossfade](podcasts.md#where-you-left-off).** Plays episodes one after another without a crossfade, and a queue that ends on one stops instead of starting a radio. *Built in* · since 0.5.9
- **[Episodes stay out of stats](podcasts.md#not-counted).** Leaves podcast episodes out of scrobbles and stats, and keeps them in the history. *Built in* · since 0.5.9

## Your library

What you keep in MixMusic and on your services, and what you did with it.

- **[Liked songs](library.md#liked-songs).** Keeps the songs you like from every service in one list that you can search and sort. *Library › Liked, Sidebar* · since 0.1.0
- **[Likes reach the service](library.md#liked-songs).** Signed in, a like also saves the Spotify track or likes the YouTube Music track on the service. *Player bar, A track's menu* · since 0.2.0
- **[Mixes](library.md#mixes).** Makes playlists of your own that hold tracks of every service together. *Library › Mixes, Sidebar* · since 0.1.0
- **[Copy a playlist into a mix](library.md#mixes).** Turns a playlist of a service into a mix that any track can join. *A playlist's More menu* · since 0.1.0
- **[Put songs in order](library.md#mixes).** Drag a song onto another, or press Alt with the arrow keys, to reorder a mix or a playlist of yours. *A mix's page* · since 0.5.11
- **[Keep your own order of mixes](library.md#the-order-of-your-mixes).** Mixes stay in the order you drag them to, and changing a mix does not move it. *Library › Mixes, Sidebar* · since 0.5.12
- **[Search and sort mixes](library.md#the-order-of-your-mixes).** Finds mixes by name and sorts them by your order, last changed, date added, title or track count. *Library › Mixes, Sidebar* · since 0.5.12
- **[Search and sort a list's songs](library.md#liked-songs).** Searches inside a playlist, a mix or your liked songs, and sorts them by title, artist or length. *A playlist's page, Library › Liked* · since 0.2.0
- **[Sort by plays](library.md#liked-songs).** Puts the songs you played most in MixMusic first in any list of songs, as your history counts them. *A list's Sort menu* · since 0.5.11
- **[Disliked songs list](library.md#disliked-songs).** Lists the songs you disliked, newest first, with search and sort, and lets you take a dislike back. *Library › Disliked* · since 0.5.9
- **[Your YouTube Music library](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Shows your liked songs, playlists, albums, artists and saved songs on YouTube Music once you sign in. *Library › YouTube Music* · since 0.2.0
- **[Your Spotify library](library.md#spotify-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Shows your playlists, saved albums, followed artists and liked songs on Spotify once you sign in. *Library › Spotify* · since 0.1.0
- **[Search and sort service playlists](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Searches and sorts your Spotify and YouTube Music playlists by the service's order, title or track count. *Library › Spotify, Library › YouTube Music* · since 0.5.12
- **[Save albums and playlists](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Saves albums and playlists, and follows artists, on Spotify or YouTube Music from their pages. *An album's page, A playlist's page, An artist's page* · since 0.2.0
- **[Make YouTube Music playlists](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Makes playlists on YouTube Music, adds tracks of either service to them and takes tracks out of your own. *Library › YouTube Music, A track's menu* · since 0.3.0
- **[Already in this playlist](library.md#mixes).** Says when a song is in the playlist already, and Add anyway adds it a second time. *A track's menu* · since 0.5.11
- **[Add to a Spotify playlist](library.md#spotify-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Adds a Spotify track to one of your playlists or to one you collaborate on. *A track's menu* · since 0.5.11
- **[Delete or remove a YouTube Music playlist](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Deletes a playlist you made on YouTube Music, or takes a saved one out of your library. *A playlist's More menu, Library › YouTube Music* · since 0.5.8
- **[Playlist suggestions](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Offers five songs YouTube Music suggests for a playlist of yours, each with Add, and Refresh for five more. *A playlist's page* · since 0.5.11
- **[Add to library without liking](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Saves a YouTube Music song to your library without liking it, so your likes stay as they are. *A track's menu* · since 0.5.10
- **[Collaborate on playlists](library.md#collaborating-on-playlists).** Invites others to add to a playlist of yours on YouTube Music or Spotify, and lists the ones you collaborate on. *A playlist's More menu, Library › Spotify, Library › YouTube Music* · since 0.5.11
- **[Share a mix as a YouTube Music playlist](library.md#collaborating-on-playlists).** Makes an unlisted YouTube Music playlist from a mix and copies its link to share. *A mix's More menu* · since 0.5.11
- **[Long playlists](library.md#long-playlists).** Reads playlists of up to 20,000 tracks, showing the first ones at once and saying why a count is above the rows. *Built in* · since 0.5.9
- **[All of a large library](library.md#youtube-music-playlists-albums-and-artists).** Shows every playlist, album, artist and podcast you keep on a service, page by page as it loads. *Library › Spotify, Library › YouTube Music* · since 0.5.12
- **[Pin to top](library.md#pin-to-top).** Puts a mix, playlist, saved album or followed artist first in every list it is in, with a pin mark. *A tile's menu, A page's More menu* · since 0.5.12
- **[Pins in the sidebar](library.md#pin-to-top).** Shows pinned playlists, albums and artists under Liked songs and Downloads, which you can drag or hide. *Sidebar* · since 0.5.12
- **[Hide from library](library.md#hide-from-library).** Takes a playlist or saved album out of MixMusic's library without changing it on the service, and Hidden shows it again. *A tile's menu, Library › Spotify, Library › YouTube Music* · since 0.5.12
- **[Edit tabs](library.md#the-library-page).** Moves and hides the tabs of the Library in the order you like. *Library* · since 0.5.12
- **[Folders for playlists](library.md#folders).** Groups your mixes and your service playlists in folders, one level deep, by moving or dragging them in. *Sidebar* · since 0.5.12
- **[Folder header and menu](library.md#folders).** A folder shows its name and size, opens and closes with a click, and plays, renames or ungroups from its menu. *Sidebar* · since 0.5.12
- **[Tags](library.md#tags).** Puts tags of your own on mixes, saved playlists and albums, and albums on this computer, as many as you like on each. *A tile's menu, A page's More menu* · since 0.5.12
- **[Manage tags](library.md#tags).** Makes, renames, recolours, orders and deletes tags, and tags many playlists and albums at once. *Library › Tags* · since 0.5.12
- **[Filter by tag](library.md#tags).** A row of chips picks the tags shown, matching any or all, and Library › Tags lists everything tagged. *Library › Mixes, Library › Tags* · since 0.5.12
- **[Show tag filters](library.md#tags).** Shows or hides the row of tag chips above Library › Mixes. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type* · since 0.5.12
- **[Change a mix's cover](library.md#a-mixs-cover).** Picks an image of yours or the cover of one of its songs as the cover of a mix. *A mix's menu, A mix's page* · since 0.5.11
- **[Follow artists without an account](library.md#artists-you-follow).** Follows artists inside MixMusic when you are signed out, and on their service when you are signed in. *An artist's page, A tile's menu* · since 0.5.10
- **[Followed artists](library.md#artists-you-follow).** Lists the artists you follow in MixMusic and on each service you signed in to. *Library › Artists* · since 0.5.10
- **[Header in the top bar](library.md#the-header-that-stays).** Scrolled down a playlist, album, artist or liked songs, the top bar shows its cover, name, Play, Shuffle and Download. *Top bar* · since 0.5.9
- **[History](library.md#history-and-recently-played).** Lists the last 300 tracks you played, and Clear history forgets them. *Library › History* · since 0.1.0
- **[Stats](library.md#stats).** Shows how many tracks and minutes you played this week, this month or all time, with your top artists and tracks. *Stats* · since 0.1.0
- **[Minutes per day](library.md#stats).** Shows the minutes you listened each day as a chart, and also as a table. *Stats* · since 0.2.0
- **[Your year](library.md#your-year).** Looks back at a year of your history: minutes, top five songs, artists and albums, your busiest month and new artists. *Stats* · since 0.5.11
- **[Year card images](library.md#your-year).** Saves your year as a picture card in your theme's colours, square or tall for a story. *Stats* · since 0.5.11

## Sound

What changes how the music sounds.

- **[Your own equalizer presets](audio-engine.md#the-equalizer).** Saves your bands and sound settings under a name, and renames, deletes, exports and imports presets as JSON files. *Settings › Playback › Equalizer › Preset* · since 0.5.12
- **[Simple equalizer mode](audio-engine.md#the-equalizer).** Moves the ten bands with three sliders: Bass, Mid and Treble. *Settings › Playback › Equalizer › Simple mode* · since 0.5.12
- **[Auto headroom](audio-engine.md#the-equalizer).** Turns the preamp down by as much as the bands boost, so no note comes out louder than it went in. *Settings › Playback › Equalizer › Auto headroom* · since 0.5.12
- **[Sound effects switch](audio-engine.md#the-equalizer).** Turns the equalizer, reverb, echo, crossfeed and stereo width off at once and back on, keeping their settings. *Settings › Playback › Equalizer › Sound effects* · since 0.5.12
- **[Headphone correction](audio-engine.md#headphones).** Corrects the sound of your headphones with a profile from AutoEq's list of over 8,800, searched by model or source. *Settings › Playback › Equalizer › Headphones* · since 0.5.12
- **[Import a headphone correction file](audio-engine.md#headphones).** Reads a ParametricEQ.txt file of up to 20 filters and says which line it can't read. *Settings › Playback › Equalizer › Headphones › Import file…* · since 0.5.12
- **[Crossfade](audio-engine.md#crossfade-and-the-next-track).** Fades one track into the next, overlapping them by 1 to 12 seconds. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Crossfade* · since 0.1.0
- **[Keep albums gapless](audio-engine.md#crossfade-and-the-next-track).** Plays the songs of one album straight on without a crossfade, as live and concept albums are meant to run. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Keep albums gapless* · since 0.5.12
- **[Gapless track changes](audio-engine.md#crossfade-and-the-next-track).** Starts the next track on the sample after the last one when crossfade is off, with no silence between. *Built in* · since 0.5.12
- **[Even loudness](audio-engine.md#even-loudness).** Turns loud YouTube Music tracks down to one level from their first note, so none jumps out. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Even loudness* · since 0.5.6
- **[Volume levelling](audio-engine.md#even-loudness).** Squeezes loud and quiet moments closer together as they play, and lifts quiet recordings. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Volume levelling* · since 0.1.0
- **[Volume slider with even steps](audio-engine.md#even-loudness).** Gives the volume slider even steps of loudness, so each press of the volume keys changes it by about the same. *Player bar* · since 0.5.6
- **[Volume curve](audio-engine.md#even-loudness).** Chooses how the slider maps to loudness: Natural for even steps of loudness, or Linear for an even growth in level. *Settings › Playback › Volume › Volume curve* · since 0.5.11
- **[Output device](playing-music.md#the-output-device).** Plays music on headphones or a DAC while calls and system sounds stay on the speakers. *Player bar, Settings › Playback › Sound › Output device* · since 0.5.11
- **[5.1 surround](audio-engine.md#surround-sound).** Plays the 5.1 surround mix of tracks that have one, on a 5.1 device or mixed down to stereo. *Player bar, Settings › Playback › Sound › Play 5.1 surround when a track has it* · since 0.5.9
- **[Reverb](audio-engine.md#reverb-and-echo).** Plays the music in a room: a small room, a hall, a plate or a large space, at a mix you set. *Settings › Playback › Reverb* · since 0.5.12
- **[Echo](audio-engine.md#reverb-and-echo).** Repeats the music with a slapback, a quarter note or a long tail, and sets its time, feedback and mix. *Settings › Playback › Echo* · since 0.5.12
- **[Balance](audio-engine.md#balance-mono-crossfeed-and-width).** Moves the sound toward the left or right ear, with a rest at the centre. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Balance* · since 0.5.12
- **[Mono](audio-engine.md#balance-mono-crossfeed-and-width).** Plays both channels in each ear, for one ear or one earbud. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Mono* · since 0.5.12
- **[Crossfeed](audio-engine.md#balance-mono-crossfeed-and-width).** Mixes a little of each channel into the other ear, so music on headphones sounds less wide. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Crossfeed* · since 0.5.12
- **[Stereo width](audio-engine.md#balance-mono-crossfeed-and-width).** Narrows the stereo image to mono or widens it to twice as wide. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Stereo width* · since 0.5.12
- **[Data saver](playing-music.md#data-saver).** Streams smaller files of YouTube Music to use less data. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Quality* · since 0.1.0
- **[Play audio as your account](playing-music.md#play-audio-as-your-account).** Asks for each track's audio as your signed-in account, so YouTube Music Premium gets its higher bitrate. *Settings › Playback › Sound › Play audio as your account* · since 0.5.11
- **[Visualizer](audio-engine.md#the-visualizer).** Draws the spectrum of what plays as bars over the cover, and plugins read the same analyser. *Now playing* · since 0.1.0
- **[Own audio engine](audio-engine.md#decoding).** Decodes YouTube Music's audio itself and plays it on your audio device, with no FFmpeg and no system codec. *Built in* · since 0.5.0
- **[Tracks that only come in AAC](audio-engine.md#decoding).** Plays the few YouTube Music tracks that exist only in AAC, which used to fail. *Built in* · since 0.5.6
- **[Steady audio under load](audio-engine.md#the-output-on-each-system).** Keeps the music from stuttering while a game or a browser keeps the computer busy. *Built in* · Windows only · since 0.5.2
- **[Linux sound output](audio-engine.md#the-output-on-each-system).** Plays through PulseAudio, which PipeWire serves too, or ALSA, with no GStreamer needed. *Built in* · Linux only · since 0.5.0

- **[Control from a key or a script](desktop.md#control-from-a-key-or-a-script).** Plays, pauses, skips, mutes, sets the volume, shuffles and repeats from a Stream Deck key, a macro or a script, with no window coming forward. *Command line* · since 0.5.12

- **[Overlay data for your own page](stream-overlay.md#make-your-own-overlay).** Tells what plays as JSON, the cover and Server-Sent Events on this computer, for an overlay of your own. *Settings › Desktop › Now playing for overlays* · since 0.5.11
- **[Windows proxy](streams.md#proxies).** Connects through the proxy set in Windows' settings, as browsers do, and follows it as VPN and proxy apps turn it on and off. *Built in* · Windows only · since 0.5.9

- **[Follow the computer's light or dark mode](look-and-feel.md#follow-the-computers-light-or-dark-mode).** Switches between a light theme and a dark theme of your choice as the computer changes between light and dark. *Settings › Appearance › Theme › Follow the computer* · since 0.2.0
- **[Accent from the artwork](look-and-feel.md#the-accent-and-the-artwork).** Takes the accent colour from the cover of what plays and changes it with each track. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Follow the artwork* · since 0.1.0
- **[Accent colour of your own](look-and-feel.md#the-accent-and-the-artwork).** Sets an accent colour you choose, used while nothing plays or when Follow the artwork is off. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Accent colour* · since 0.1.0
- **[Colours from a file](look-and-feel.md#colours-from-a-file).** Wears the colours pywal or matugen make of your wallpaper, or a file of your own, and changes colour as the file does. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Colours from a file* · since 0.5.9
- **[Make your own theme](themes.md#make-one).** Makes a theme from a folder with a theme.json that sets colours, typefaces and corners, and shows it with every layout. *Settings › Appearance › Theme › open themes folder* · since 0.1.0
- **[Zoom](look-and-feel.md#density-text-size-and-zoom).** Makes the whole interface larger or smaller, from 80% to 200%, with keys of its own, in the mini player too. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Zoom, Keyboard* · since 0.5.6
- **[Text size](look-and-feel.md#density-text-size-and-zoom).** Makes the text alone larger or smaller, from 85% to 130%. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Text size* · since 0.1.0
- **[Density](look-and-feel.md#density-text-size-and-zoom).** Fits more rows on the screen in compact density, with rows, sidebar and player bar taking less room. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Density* · since 0.1.0
- **[Icons only in the sidebar](look-and-feel.md#density-text-size-and-zoom).** Shows the sidebar's pages as icons alone. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Icons only in the sidebar* · since 0.5.0
- **[Fold the sidebar](getting-started.md#the-sidebar).** Folds the sidebar to its icons with a button or Ctrl+B, and by itself in a narrow window. *Sidebar, Keyboard* · since 0.1.0
- **[Wavy progress bar](look-and-feel.md#density-text-size-and-zoom).** Draws the played part of the seek bar as a gentle wave that moves while music plays and eases flat on pause. *Settings › Appearance › Colour and type › Wavy progress bar* · since 0.5.11
- **[Reduced motion](look-and-feel.md#reduced-motion).** Moves less while the system asks for less motion: theme effects, springs, the visualizer and the wavy bar hold still. *Built in* · since 0.1.0
- **[Title bar follows the theme](getting-started.md#the-window).** Draws the window's title bar and buttons light or dark as the theme is. *Built in* · since 0.5.0
- **Sharp covers on dense screens.** Fetches covers and music videos at the display's own pixels, so they stay sharp on Retina and other high-density screens. *Built in* · since 0.5.12
