        One player for both services

        Every page of MixMusic works across Spotify and YouTube Music. Pick one to see it.

        One search box for both services

        Suggestions come as you type, and the songs, albums, artists and playlists of Spotify and YouTube Music land in the same lists, each with a mark of where it comes from.

        • A disc for Spotify
        • A square for YouTube Music

        YouTube Music needs no account. Spotify joins the same search once you sign in with an app of your own; the guide takes two minutes.

        Tracks of both, in one queue

        Play next or add to the queue from any track, album or playlist, then drag to reorder. Shuffle, repeat and crossfade work across both services, and a radio carries on when the queue runs out.

        With Premium, Spotify tracks play in MixMusic or on a Spotify Connect device. Without it, MixMusic plays their recording from YouTube Music.

        Liked songs and mixes of every service

        Like songs from either service and keep them together. Mixes hold tracks of both, a Spotify or YouTube Music playlist imports into one, and you can search and sort inside any of them.

        Your history and listening stats, day by day, sit beside them.

        Find what you have not heard yet

        New albums and singles, the charts of your country, and dozens of moods and genres, each opening its playlists. Under now playing, songs, playlists and artists like what you hear.

        Explore comes from YouTube Music, so it needs no account.

        Lyrics that follow the song

        Synced lyrics from LRCLIB and YouTube Music light up line by line, on now playing or beside any page, or read them whole as plain text.

        Tune the sound with a 10-band equalizer and its presets, volume levelling and crossfade. What you played is cached for next time.

        Make it yours

        The whole look is a theme. Ten ship with the app, and the community makes more.

        Mix The default: flat colour tiles and giant lowercase on true black.

        Make a theme

        A theme is a folder with a theme.json: eighteen colours, the fonts and a corner radius. MixMusic draws everything with them, so a theme keeps working as the app changes. Let it follow your computer, with one theme for light mode and one for dark.

        Add a plugin

        Plugins are ES modules that add pages, commands, track actions and widgets through a typed API, and install from the marketplace in one click, like a sleep timer or a spectrum on now playing. Every plugin can use the whole API, and MixMusic tells you so before you enable one.

        What it needs

        YouTube Music
        yt-dlp, which MixMusic installs for you from Settings › Playback, and a JavaScript runtime on your PATH: Deno, Node.js, Bun or QuickJS.
        Spotify
        An app of your own from the Spotify developer dashboard, made with a Spotify account that has Premium. The guide walks through it.
        First launch
        The macOS app is not notarized yet: open it once from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. The Windows installer is not signed yet: if SmartScreen warns, choose More info › Run anyway.
        Linux
        GTK 3, and PulseAudio (which PipeWire serves too) or ALSA; signing in to YouTube Music takes WebKitGTK 4.1. On Debian and Ubuntu: sudo apt install libgtk-3-0 libpulse0 libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0

        Check each file against SHA256SUMS in the release. Older versions are on the releases page too.

        Built in the open

        MixMusic is open source under the MIT license or the GNU GPL, version 3: one small Go program built on MyGo, which draws its windows itself on the GPU, with no browser inside. Fixes, features, themes, plugins and reports of what does not work are all welcome.
