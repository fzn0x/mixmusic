          One player for both services

          Every page of MixMusic works across Spotify and YouTube Music. Pick one to see it.

          One search box for both services

          Suggestions come as you type, and the songs, albums, artists, playlists and podcasts of Spotify and YouTube Music land in the same lists, each with a mark of where it comes from. YouTube's videos, such as DJ sets and live recordings, have a list of their own.

          • A disc for Spotify
          • A square for YouTube Music

          YouTube Music needs no account. Spotify joins the same search once you sign in with an app of your own; the guide takes two minutes.

          Tracks of both, in one queue

          Play next or add to the queue from any track, album or playlist, then drag to reorder. Shuffle, repeat, crossfade and speed work across both services, a radio carries on when the queue runs out, and the songs you dislike are passed over.

          With Premium, Spotify tracks play in MixMusic, one after another without a gap, or on a Spotify Connect device. Without it, MixMusic plays their recording from YouTube Music. A cast button sends what plays to a Chromecast or a Google speaker on your network.

          Liked songs, mixes, podcasts and downloads

          Like songs from either service and keep them together. Mixes hold tracks of both, and a Spotify or YouTube Music playlist imports into one. Library › Podcasts keeps the shows you follow, and Library › Downloads what plays without the network.

          Your history and listening stats, day by day, sit beside them. Playlists of up to 20,000 tracks load in full.

          Find what you have not heard yet

          New albums and singles, the charts of your country, and dozens of moods and genres, each opening its playlists. Under now playing, songs, playlists and artists like what you hear.

          Explore comes from YouTube Music, so it needs no account. YouTube's live radio channels play too.

          Lyrics that follow the song

          Synced lyrics from Unison, LRCLIB and YouTube Music light up line by line, or word by word where they're timed so, on now playing or beside any page. Translate them, or show Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Russian and many other scripts in Latin letters.

          Tune the sound with a 10-band equalizer and its presets, volume levelling and crossfade. Tracks YouTube Music has a 5.1 mix of play in surround on a 5.1 system.

          Your own music comes along

          Last.fm gets every track you hear, and Discord shows what plays on your profile.

            Lyrics in your language

            Turn on translate beside the lyrics and each line shows its translation under it. Japanese can show in romaji, and Korean, Chinese, Russian, Hindi, Thai and other scripts in Latin letters, made on your computer. Lyrics timed word by word light each word as it's sung.

          • Podcasts

            Shows and episodes from YouTube Music and Spotify, in search and in Library › Podcasts. An episode starts where you left it, on another device too, with a speed of its own.

          • Downloads

            Download songs, albums, playlists, mixes and your liked songs, and they play without the network. Songs added to a downloaded playlist later download on their own.

          • Layouts

            Blend, the new default, Expressive, Crate and Tiles, MixMusic's look until now. Each sets shapes, density, headings and motion apart from the theme, and every theme shows in every layout.

            See them below
          • 5.1

            5.1 surround

            A track YouTube Music has a 5.1 mix of shows a 5.1 button by the player: six channels on a 5.1 system, mixed down to stereo on headphones.

          • Casting

            Send what plays to a Chromecast, a Google Home or Nest speaker, or a TV with Chromecast built in. The queue stays in MixMusic, and the device's own buttons work.

          • A player around the notch

            On a MacBook with a notch, the black around the camera widens with the cover and bars that move as it plays. Click it for the player, the progress and the line of lyrics sung now, over any app, full screen too.

          • 1.25×

            Speed

            From half speed to three times, at the song's own pitch, from the speed button by the player or the command palette.

          • Global shortcuts

            Keys of your own that play, skip, change the volume or like a song from any app, recorded in Settings › Shortcuts.

          • Dislikes

            The thumbs-down passes over a song from then on, in the queue and in radios. Signed in to YouTube Music, your dislikes go there too. Songs YouTube labels as made with AI can be skipped as well.

          • Live streams and music videos

            YouTube's 24/7 radio channels play live, and music videos can show their picture on the now playing page, muted and in step with the sound.

          Make it yours

          A layout sets the shapes, the density and the headings; a theme sets the colours, the type and the corners. Choose any of each in Settings › Appearance.

          Mix, in the Blend layout The default: true black and greys, with cyan for everything live.

          Layout

          Theme

          Each theme is shown in Blend, and each layout in Mix; every theme works in every layout. With Follow the artwork on, as it is at first, the accent takes the colour of the cover playing. More themes, as Neon Nights and Paper Mint, install from the marketplace.

          Make a theme

          A theme is a folder with a theme.json: eighteen colours, the fonts and a corner radius. MixMusic draws everything with them, in every layout, so a theme keeps working as the app changes. Let it follow your computer, with one theme for light mode and one for dark, or wear the colours pywal or matugen make of your wallpaper.

          Add a plugin

          Plugins are ES modules that add pages, commands, track actions and widgets through a typed API, and install from the marketplace in one click, like a sleep timer or a spectrum on now playing. Every plugin can use the whole API, and MixMusic tells you so before you enable one.

          What it needs

          YouTube Music
          yt-dlp, which MixMusic installs for you from Settings › Playback, and a JavaScript runtime on your PATH: Deno, Node.js, Bun or QuickJS.
          Spotify
          An app of your own from the Spotify developer dashboard, made with a Spotify account that has Premium. The guide walks through it.
          First launch
          The macOS app is not notarized yet: open it once from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. The Windows installer is not signed yet: if SmartScreen warns, choose More info › Run anyway.
          Linux
          GTK 3, and PulseAudio (which PipeWire serves too) or ALSA; signing in to YouTube Music takes WebKitGTK 4.1. The Flatpak needs only Flatpak, and brings the rest with it. On Debian and Ubuntu:
          sudo apt install libgtk-3-0 libpulse0 libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0

          Check each file against SHA256SUMS in the release. Older versions are on the releases page too. Troubleshooting helps when something doesn't work.

          Built in the open

          MixMusic is open source under the MIT license or the GNU GPL, version 3: one Go program built on MyGo, which draws its windows itself on the GPU, with no browser inside. Fixes, features, themes, plugins and reports of what does not work are all welcome.
